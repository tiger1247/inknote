## Short description
A pen-first notebook for Windows. Write with a pen, mouse or finger on ruled, grid or dotted pages; add highlighter, shapes and text; export crisp vector PDFs. Free, offline, and no account needed.

## Description
InkNote is a fast, pen-first notebook for Windows that feels like writing on paper.

Write naturally
• Smooth, pressure-sensitive ink that keeps up with your pen, with no jitter or lag
• Works with Windows pens, Surface, and pen tablets such as XP-Pen and Wacom (Windows Ink), plus mouse and touch
• Pens, pencil and highlighters in your own colours and thicknesses
• Hold the pen still to snap a stroke into a straight line

Organise your notes
• Subjects and pages in a sidebar, with search across titles and typed text
• Typed text boxes, pictures (paste or drag & drop), lines, arrows, rectangles and ellipses
• Lasso to move, resize, recolour, copy, duplicate or delete anything
• Insert space to make room in the middle of a page

Paper your way
• Plain, ruled, narrow, wide, grid or dotted paper in 11 colours
• Light and dark themes. On dark paper, dark ink brightens automatically.
• Zoom with Ctrl + wheel, touchpad pinch or two fingers; full-screen focus mode

Share and stay safe
• Export a page, a subject or all notes to PDF with vector ink and real text, in A4, Letter or one long page (great for reading on a phone)
• Saved automatically as you write, with daily backups and one-click backup/restore
• Everything stays on your PC. No account, no ads, no tracking.

## Notes for certification (max 500 chars)
Electron (Win32) desktop notebook packaged as MSIX. runFullTrust is required because it is a Win32 desktop app; it reads and writes only the user's notebook in Documents\InkNote and files the user picks in Save/Open dialogs (PDF export, backup/restore). No network access, no sign-in. Test: open InkNote, draw with mouse/pen, add a page, press Ctrl+E to export a PDF.
