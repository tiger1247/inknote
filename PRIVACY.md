# InkNote Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

InkNote is a notebook app that runs entirely on your Windows PC.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** InkNote has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never connects to the internet. The developer never receives your notes or any information about you.

## Your notes

- Your notebook is saved on your PC in `Documents\InkNote\notebook.json`.
- Daily backups are kept in `Documents\InkNote\backups` for 30 days.
- PDFs and `.inknote` backup files are saved only where you choose.
- Window size and position are stored in the app's local settings folder.

If your Documents folder is synced by a service such as OneDrive, that service's own privacy terms apply to the synced copy.

## Microsoft Store

If you install InkNote from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and installation under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Children

InkNote does not collect information from anyone, including children.
